# Kinoteka v1.2 Stable HF56 — Whole-row Sorting Menu

Small correction over HF55: the sorting dropdown had 258px maximum height,
which showed the next option only partially. Its cap is now exactly 244px:
8 × 29px full rows + 10px vertical padding + 2px border.

Scrolling snaps to item boundaries, so subsequent scroll positions also land on
complete rows. The dark 5px scrollbar, violet selection, dropdown width, and
all 14 sort options are retained. **The reset control is not changed at all.**
The window, taskbar hot edge, personal databases, and poster folders are untouched.
No new dependencies or network access are required.
